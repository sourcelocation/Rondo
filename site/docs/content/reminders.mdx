Reminders are off until you turn on **Daily reminder** in **Settings › Reminders**:

- **Remind me at**: the time of day. It applies to all your devices.
- **On this device**: a notification here. Your browser asks to allow notifications first.
- **By email**: an email too, for days when you're away from your devices.

## When they come

At most once a day, at your time, and only when cards are due and you haven't studied that day yet. Each one says how many cards are waiting.

If you ignore a week of reminders in a row, they pause and Rondo tells you. Studying again turns them back on.

## In a browser

Notifications show while the browser is running, even without Rondo open. On iPhone and iPad, add Rondo to your Home Screen first (**Share › Add to Home Screen**), and turn reminders on from there.

<Callout type="info">
  If you blocked notifications for Rondo, **On this device** can't turn on. Allow them in the browser's site settings,
  then try again.
</Callout>

To stop reminders on one device, turn off **On this device** there. Signing out turns them off too.
