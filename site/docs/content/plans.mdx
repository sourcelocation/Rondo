Free includes everything for studying on your own and with others: sync, Anki import, sharing, Discover, and AI assistants running on your computer. Pro adds the rest.

| Feature                                        | Free     | Pro      |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------- | -------- |
| Study, edit, import, sync, share, Discover     | Included | Included |
| Let people edit decks you share                | —        | Included |
| AI assistants connected through Rondo's server | —        | Included |
| AI assistants on your computer (`rondo-mcp`)   | Included | Included |
| Storage for pictures and sounds                | 200 MB   | 5 GB     |

## Manage your subscription

Open **Profile › Subscription** to subscribe monthly or yearly, and **Manage subscription** to change or cancel it. A subscription bought in Rondo's iPhone or Android app is managed in that store.

When Pro ends, people you share with as editors can still study, and pictures and sounds already stored stay; new ones need room under the Free limit.
