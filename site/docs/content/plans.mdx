Free includes everything for studying on your own and with others: sync, Anki import, sharing, Discover, and AI assistants. Pro lets people edit the decks you share, with more room for pictures and sounds.

| Feature                                          | Free     | Pro      |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------- | -------- |
| Study, edit, import, sync, share, Discover       | Included | Included |
| AI assistants, through Rondo or on your computer | Included | Included |
| Let people edit decks you share                  | —        | Included |
| Storage for pictures and sounds                  | 200 MB   | 10 GB    |

## Manage your subscription

Open **Settings › Plan**, choose **Upgrade to Pro** in the menu under your name, or choose something shown with a lock, to subscribe monthly or yearly; **Manage subscription** there changes or cancels it. A subscription bought in Rondo's iPhone or Android app is managed in that store.

When Pro ends, people you share with as editors can still study, and pictures and sounds already stored stay; new ones need room under the Free limit.
