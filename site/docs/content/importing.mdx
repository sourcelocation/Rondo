---
title: Import from Anki
icon: Import
description: Bring your decks, review history, and media into Rondo.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Export from Anki

In Anki, open **File › Export**. Choose **Anki Deck Package** (`.apkg`) for a deck or **Anki Collection Package** (`.colpkg`) for everything. Include scheduling information and media. Both the current format and the older one ("Support older Anki versions") work.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose how to import

Open **Home › Import from Anki** (or **Settings › General › Import from Anki**).

| Option                 | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Make it editable       | On by default. Turns Anki's HTML into Rondo cards: formatting, clozes, MathJax and furigana become Rondo's own, images and sounds get fields of their own. Standard Anki types become Rondo's built-in ones. |
| Include review history | On by default. Brings every past review, for [Progress](/studying#progress) and a schedule fitted to you. Off brings only where each card stands.                                                            |
| Deck                   | Where the imported decks go: the top level, or inside one of your decks.                                                                                                                                     |

With **Make it editable** off, cards keep Anki's own HTML and look exactly as they did in Anki, but can't be edited in Rondo. You can change your mind later: open the deck and choose **Convert to Rondo format**, which makes an editable copy and keeps your progress.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the file

Choose **Choose file** and pick the package. It is read on your device; only your decks, notes and media sync afterwards, as usual.

When it's done, Rondo says how many notes and media files came in, and how many notes didn't fit (a field over 64 KB, say) and were left out.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Image occlusion" type="info">
  Anki's Image Occlusion notes become Rondo's: rectangles, ellipses and polygons each become a mask.
</Callout>

Notes keep their tags, so a deck organized by them can be filtered by them in [Browse](/search#tags). Anki's own `marked` tag becomes a [mark](/organizing#marks) on the note's cards, and `leech` is left out.

Importing the same package twice makes a second copy; delete the first from **Decks** if you don't need it.
