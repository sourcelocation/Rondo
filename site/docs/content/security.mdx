Open **Profile › Security**. Some changes need a recent sign-in: Rondo then sends a code to your email to confirm it's you.

## Authenticator app

A passkey already protects you. Turning on an authenticator app adds a second step to signing in with an email code, Google or Apple, so someone with your email still can't get in.

1. Choose **Turn on**, and scan the code with an authenticator app (or type the key).
2. Enter the code it shows.
3. Save the **recovery codes**. Each works once instead of the app; they aren't shown again.

**New recovery codes** replaces the old ones. **Turn off** removes the second step.

## Lost your authenticator?

On the sign-in screen, after the email code, choose **Lost your authenticator?**. Rondo emails you a code, and once you enter it, the second step is removed **a week later**. The wait stops a stranger with your email from doing the same.

Until then, signing in with the authenticator app or a recovery code cancels it, and so does **Cancel it** in **Profile › Security**. Rondo emails you when it's scheduled and when it's done.

## Signed-in devices

**Signed in on** lists every device and browser with your account. **Sign out** ends one; **Sign out everywhere else** ends all but this one.

## Change your email

Enter the new address and choose **Change email**. A code goes to the new address; entering it moves your account there, and the old address gets an email saying so.

## Linked accounts

Link Google or Apple to sign in with them as well. **Unlink** removes one; signing in with an email code always works.
