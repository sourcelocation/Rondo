import { GraduationCap, NotebookPen } from "lucide-react";

Already have Anki decks? [Import them](/importing) instead.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Rondo

Open [the web app](https://rondo.matthewsource.com/app/) or a Rondo app. It works without an account: your decks stay on that device. Sign in when you want them on your other devices too.

</Step>
<Step>

### Make a deck

On **Decks**, choose **New deck**, give it a name, and choose **Save**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a note

Open the deck and choose **Add note**. Keep the type **Basic** and fill in the front and back:

| Field | Example                       |
| ----- | ----------------------------- |
| Front | What is the capital of Japan? |
| Back  | Tokyo                         |

Choose **Add**. Rondo turns the note into a card; the editor stays open so you can add another.

</Step>
<Step>

### Study it

Back on the deck, choose **Study**. Think of the answer, choose **Show answer**, then say how it went.

Come back tomorrow: **Decks** shows how many cards are due in each deck.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Start small" type="idea">
  Keep each card to one question. A few clear cards are easier to study than one card with a whole paragraph to
  remember.
</Callout>
