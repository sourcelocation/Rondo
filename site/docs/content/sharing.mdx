Open a deck of yours and choose **Share**. Sharing covers the deck and its sub-decks, and needs a connection.

<Callout type="info">
  Everyone keeps their own progress. Editors change the notes for everyone; nobody changes anyone else's schedule.
</Callout>

## Invite people

| Role      | Can                             |
| --------- | ------------------------------- |
| Can study | Study the deck.                 |
| Can edit  | Study, and add or change notes. |

- **Invite by email**: they get an email with a link.
- **Create a link**: anyone with the link can join, for 7 days.

An invitation opens a page showing the deck as Discover would (its description, sub-decks and a few sample cards), with **Join**. People and pending invitations are listed in the share sheet, to change their role, remove them, or revoke an invitation; removing or revoking asks first. Letting people edit needs Pro; if your Pro ends, editors can still study.

## Publish to Discover

Turn on **Publish to Discover** and the deck appears on **Discover** for anyone to find, with its description, language, and a few sample cards. You need [your profile](/profiles) set up first. Turning it off takes it off Discover; people who follow it keep it.

## Decks shared with you

They appear under **Shared with you** on **Home** and in the sidebar, with who shared them. **Leave** stops borrowing one.

On **Discover**, **Follow** adds a deck to Shared with you, and it keeps getting its author's changes. **Copy** gives you your own copy to change; it doesn't follow the deck.

Moving one of your sub-decks out of a deck others borrow asks first: they lose access to it.

Decks on Discover follow the [community rules](/rules). **Report** on a deck or profile tells moderators about one that doesn't.

If an author deletes a deck you borrow, or their account, you keep it: it's marked **Removed by its author**, can't change any more, and you can still study or copy it.
