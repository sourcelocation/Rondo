Signing in for the first time asks you to set up your profile: a **username**, a **display name** and a **flag**. It starts filled in (from your Google or Apple name, and your country) so one tap is enough. You can change it later in **Settings › Account › Edit profile**. Sharing and publishing need it, so people know who's sharing.

## Username

Your username is your address: `rondo.matthewsource.com/u/yourname`. It has 3 to 20 lowercase letters, digits and underscores, and is yours alone.

- You can change it once every 30 days. Choosing it the first time doesn't count.
- An old username stays reserved for you for 30 days, and its address leads to your new one.

## Display name and flag

The display name is how you appear next to your decks: any name, in any script. The flag is optional, and can be any country or none.

## Your public page

**View profile** there, or **Your profile** in the menu under your name, opens it. Anyone can see:

- your names, flag, and when you joined;
- whether you have Pro;
- your decks on Discover;
- your activity: reviews, days studied, streaks, minutes, cards learned, and a calendar of the year.

To hide your activity, turn on **Hide my activity** in **Settings › Account**. Your names and decks on Discover stay visible.

Profiles follow the [community rules](/rules). A restricted account's profile isn't shown to others.
