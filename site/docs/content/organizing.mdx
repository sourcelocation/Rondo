## Decks inside decks

A deck can hold notes and other decks. **Decks** lists yours as a tree, with what's shared with you below.

- **New deck** makes one at the top; a deck's **…** menu has **New sub-deck**.
- **Move to…** puts a deck inside another, or at the top. On a computer, you can also drag decks: between two to reorder, onto one to put it inside.
- **Copy** makes a copy of a deck, its sub-decks and notes, with your progress.
- **Delete** moves a deck to **Recently deleted**, at the bottom of **Decks**, for 30 days. **Restore** brings it back.

Each deck's ring shows how much of it you've learned.

## Levels

Some subjects build on themselves: HSK 1 before HSK 2. Turn on **Levels** in a deck's settings, and its sub-decks open one after another, in their order.

Each level after the first says what opens it: **Opens after 100% of HSK 1**, say. Change the percentage with − and + (50 to 100). A locked level's ring shows how far you are towards opening it, counting every level before it, so the first card you learn already moves it.

Locked levels show a lock. **Open now** opens one whatever the requirement. Reviews never wait for levels; only new cards come from open levels.

Levels belong to the deck, so everyone you share it with learns in the same order, each at their own pace.

## Smart decks

A smart deck is a set of filters kept: the cards they find, from any deck, studied like a deck. Make one in [Browse](/search): set the filters, then **Keep as smart deck**. It shows on **Decks** under **Smart decks**, and its page has **Edit rules**, which opens Browse with its filters to change them.

Its cards stay in their decks. Each is scheduled by its own deck, and what you study in a smart deck counts toward that deck's day too. A smart deck has its own **New cards a day** and **Reviews a day**; a locked level gives it no new cards, as anywhere else. Smart decks are yours alone: they aren't shared with a deck, nor published.

## Marks

A mark flags a card for attention: set it while studying or in [Browse](/search), and find marked notes there later.

**Suspend** keeps a note's cards out of study until you unsuspend them; nothing is lost.
